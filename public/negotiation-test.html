🔄 Complete Negotiation Flow Test

📝 Step-by-Step Test:

  1. Login as a consumer (Trisa Dominic)
  2. Visit any product category (vegetables/fruits/grains)
  3. Click "Deal" button on any product
  4. Fill negotiation form with your offer
  5. Submit the deal (checks subscription points)
  6. Check farmer's email for negotiation notification
  7. Farmer clicks Accept/Decline in email
  8. Consumer gets notification of farmer's response

  • Deal creation works in all updated categories
  • Email sent to farmer with Accept/Decline links
